## Workshop Proceedings (peer reviewed)

Proceedings of the AIWolfDial workshops co-located with INLG. Each volume contains the contest summary paper and papers by participating teams and related research.

### AIWolfDial 2025 (INLG 2025, Hanoi)

[Proceedings (ACL Anthology)](https://aclanthology.org/volumes/2025.aiwolfdial-1/)

- [AIWolfDial 2025: Summary of Natural Language Division of 7th International AIWolf Contest](https://aclanthology.org/2025.aiwolfdial-1.1/). Yoshinobu Kano, Neo Watanabe, Yuya Harada, Yuto Sahashi, Claus Aranha, Daisuke Katagami, Kei Harada, Michimasa Inaba, Takeshi Ito, Hirotaka Osawa, Takashi Otsuki, Fujio Toriumi.
- [Towards a Strategic Werewolf AI Based on Expert Strategies in Five-Player Werewolf](https://aclanthology.org/2025.aiwolfdial-1.2/). Takuma Okada, Takeshi Ito.
- [Intent-driven AIWolf Agents with Hierarchical BDI Model and Personality](https://aclanthology.org/2025.aiwolfdial-1.3/). Yuya Harada, Yoshinobu Kano.
- [Influence of Utterance Impressions on Decision-Making in LLM-to-LLM Discussions](https://aclanthology.org/2025.aiwolfdial-1.4/). Neo Watanabe, Yoshinobu Kano.
