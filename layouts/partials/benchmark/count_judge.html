{{- if $en -}} A different kind of LLM judge from the relative judge: instead of ranking the players, it reads the game and counts events — deductions (such as verbatim repetition, ignoring a direct question, contradicting oneself, voting against one's own declaration) and additions (such as a well-grounded inference or a reply that engages with what was just said) — on a rubric of 25 items in six axes. Every event is counted once per utterance, with the quoted utterance and a reason, so the result can be checked afterwards. The judge holds one conversation per game, per kind (deduction or addition) and per character: the public log is handed over one talk round at a time, and the judge counts only that character's events.
