{{- if $en -}} Two questions answered from the game records of the agent benchmark (Agents, {{ $d.season }}), independent of any judge model: does the composition of the table change how often villagers vote at the wrong player, and do models go easy on their own model family? {{- else -}} エージェントのベンチマーク（エージェント、{{ $d.season }}）の対戦記録から、ジャッジモデルに依らずに答えられる 2 つの問いを見ます。卓の顔ぶれで村人の誤投票率は変わるか、そしてモデルは自分と同じファミリーを疑いにくいか、です。 {{- end -}}

{{ cond $en "Wrong-vote rate by the composition of the table" "卓の構成ごとの誤投票率" }}

{{- if $en -}} Models are split into top, middle and bottom tiers (about 9 each) by their rank on the Agents page, and the games are grouped by how many of the five players at the table were bottom-tier models. A wrong vote is a vote by a village-side player at someone who is not the werewolf; the rate is the share of village-side votes that were wrong. The last rows have few games, so their values fluctuate. {{- else -}} モデルをエージェントの順位で上位・中位・下位の 3 層（各 9 モデル前後）に分け、試合を「卓の 5 人のうち下位層のモデルが何人いたか」で分けます。誤投票は村人陣営のプレイヤーが人狼でない相手に入れた票で、誤投票率は村人陣営の票のうち誤投票だった割合です。下の行ほど試合数が少ないので値は振れます。 {{- end -}}

{{- if $en -}} With no bottom-tier model at the table, 55% of village-side votes still miss the werewolf; the rate rises only slowly as bottom-tier models are added and stays around 60% up to three of them. Wrong votes are mostly a property of the game (little information, four talks per day) rather than of weak players at the table. Caveat: the tiers come from the LLM judge's ranking, so a bias of the judge can leak into the split. {{- else -}} 下位層のモデルが卓に 1 人もいなくても、村人陣営の票の 55% は人狼を外しています。下位層が増えても誤投票率はゆるやかにしか上がらず、3 人までは 60% 前後です。誤投票の大半は弱いプレイヤーのせいではなく、情報の少ないゲーム（1 日 4 発言）そのものの性質と見るのが妥当です。注意: 層分けは LLM ジャッジの順位に依っているので、ジャッジの偏りが層に入り込みえます。 {{- end -}}

{{ cond $en "Do models go easy on their own family?" "自分のファミリーは疑いにくいか" }}

{{- if $en -}} For each voter (or attacking werewolf), we ask whether it chose a target of its own model family (gemma, qwen and so on) more or less often than random. If voters picked their target at random from the players alive at that moment, each family would receive votes in proportion to how many of its members were available; the table shows the votes actually received divided by that expectation (the model itself is never a candidate). 1.0 = random; below 1.0 means the family was spared. Wrong votes only for voting, all attacks for the werewolf. Families with fewer than 10 expected votes are omitted. {{- else -}} 投票者（または襲撃する人狼）が、自分と同じモデルファミリー（gemma、qwen など）の相手を無作為より多く選んだか少なく選んだかを見ます。投票者がその時点の生存者から無作為に相手を選ぶなら、各ファミリーは生存者の中での人数に比例して票を受けるはずです。実際に受けた票数をその期待値で割った比を示します（自分自身は候補に入りません）。1.0 が無作為で、1.0 を下回れば同じファミリーを避けたことになります。投票は誤投票のみ、襲撃は全件が対象です。期待票数が 10 未満のファミリーは省いています。 {{- end -}}
