{{- if $en -}} A different kind of LLM judge from the relative judge: instead of ranking the players, it reads the game and counts events — deductions (such as verbatim repetition, ignoring a direct question, contradicting oneself, voting against one's own declaration) and additions (such as a well-grounded inference or a reply that engages with what was just said) — on a rubric of 25 items in six axes. Every event is counted once per utterance, with the quoted utterance and a reason, so the result can be checked afterwards. The judge holds one conversation per game, per kind (deduction or addition) and per character: the public log is handed over one talk round at a time, and the judge counts only that character's events. Source: aiwolf-nlp-llm-judge-count. {{- else -}} 相対ジャッジとは別方式の LLM ジャッジです。参加者を順位付けする代わりに、対局を読んで事象を数えます。減点（逐語の反復、名指しの質問の無視、自己矛盾、宣言と違う投票など）と加点（根拠のある推論、直前の発言を受けた応答など）を、6 軸 25 項目のルーブリックで数えます。事象は発話 1 つにつき 1 件で、引用と理由が付くので、結果をあとから検証できます。会話は 1 試合・1 種別（減点 or 加点）・1 キャラクターごとに分け、公開ログを 1 巡ずつ渡しながら、そのキャラクターの事象だけを数えます。ソース: aiwolf-nlp-llm-judge-count。 {{- end -}}

{{- if $en -}} Both judge models fall significantly below one human rater on this benchmark ("worse"), whereas the relative judge does not (see the Relative judge page). The count judge is therefore a supplementary measure, not a replacement for the relative evaluation. Where it falls short is visible by track below: on 5-player villages the net agreement is close to the human level (about 0.6), but deductions weaken as the village grows (0.56 → 0.33 → 0.24 for gemma from 5 to 9 to 13 players), and the 2024 winter contest, which had no character profiles, is close to uncorrelated. {{- else -}} このベンチマークでは、どちらのジャッジモデルも人手の評価者 1 人を有意に下回ります（worse）。相対ジャッジは下回らないので（[相対ジャッジ](../relative_judge/)）、件数ジャッジは相対評価の置き換えではなく補助の指標という位置づけです。弱いところはトラック別に見えます。5 人村では合計の一致が 0.6 前後で人手の水準に近い一方、村が大きくなるほど減点が弱くなり（gemma で 5 人村 0.56 → 9 人村 0.33 → 13 人村 0.24）、人物設定のなかった 2024 冬季大会はほぼ無相関です。 {{- end -}}
