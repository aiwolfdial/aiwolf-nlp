{{- if $en -}} At tables made of top- and middle-tier models only, top-tier villagers are suspected less than chance; the more bottom-tier models sit at the table, the more the top-tier villagers are suspected, and the less the bottom-tier ones are. The "suspicion" of good models is thus mostly something weaker models do, plausibly by voting at the most conspicuous speaker. The rightmost group has few votes. {{- else -}} 上位・中位だけの卓では上位の村人は無差別より疑われにくく、下位のモデルが増えるほど上位の村人が疑われ、下位の村人は疑われなくなります。良いモデルが疑われるのは主に弱いモデルが混ざった卓で起きることで、目立つ発話に短絡的に票を入れている可能性があります。右端は票数が少ないので値は振れます。 {{- end -}}
